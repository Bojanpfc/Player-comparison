WEEKLY ACTIVITY POINTS IN FC MOBILE

In FC Mobile, players can earn a minimum of 1,800 Activity Points every week by participating in Division Rivals. These points are essential for progressing through Division Rivals rewards and various seasonal events.

POINTS BREAKDOWN BY MODE:

VS Attack (VSA): 450 Max Weekly Points (Requires ~15-20 matches)
Head to Head (H2H): 900 Max Weekly Points (Requires ~15-20 matches)
Manager Mode: 450 Max Weekly Points (Requires ~15-20 matches)

KEY DETAILS TO REMEMBER:

Weekly Limit: Once you hit the cap for a specific mode (e.g., 900 points in H2H), you will stop earning Activity Points from that mode until the weekly reset.

Time Commitment: To maximize your rewards, you need to play roughly 45 to 60 matches total per week across all three modes.

Efficiency: Since Manager Mode can be set to "Auto-play," many players use it to grind their 450 points while they are away from their phones.

Note: The exact number of matches may vary slightly depending on your win/loss ratio, as wins generally grant more points than draws or losses.
