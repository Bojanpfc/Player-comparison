Division Rivals Guide

Activity Points

Weekly Activity Points

In FC Mobile, players can earn a minimum of 1,800 Activity Points every week by participating in Division Rivals. These points are essential for progressing through Division Rivals rewards and various seasonal events.

Points Breakdown
VS Attack (VSA)
450 Max Weekly Points
Head to Head (H2H)
900 Max Weekly Points
Manager Mode
450 Max Weekly Points

Each mode requires roughly 15–20 matches to reach its maximum weekly points.

Key Details
Weekly Limit

Once you hit the cap for a specific mode (e.g., 900 points in H2H), you will stop earning Activity Points from that mode until the weekly reset.

Time Commitment

To maximize your rewards, you need to play roughly 45 to 60 matches total per week across all three modes.

Efficiency

Since Manager Mode can be set to "Auto-play," many players use it to grind their 450 points while they are away from their phones.

Note

The exact number of matches may vary slightly depending on your win/loss ratio, as wins generally grant more points than draws or losses.
